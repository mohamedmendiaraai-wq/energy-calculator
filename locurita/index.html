How to Reduce Your Electricity Bill in the US

Understanding appliance power usage is the first step toward saving on your monthly utility bills. High-wattage appliances running for extended periods are usually responsible for unexpected bill spikes.

Proven Energy-Saving Tips:

  • Upgrade to ENERGY STAR Appliances: Certified appliances can consume up to 30% less energy than standard models.
  • Eliminate Phantom Load: Unplug electronics when not in use. Standby power accounts for roughly 5–10% of residential energy use.
  • Use Smart Thermostats: Heating and cooling represent over 50% of the average US home's energy consumption. Adjusting temperatures automatically saves significantly.
