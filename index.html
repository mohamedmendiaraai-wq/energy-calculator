How to Calculate Appliance Electricity Consumption

To calculate how much energy your household appliance uses, follow these simple steps:

  1. Find the wattage rating of the device (usually printed on the back or bottom label).
  2. Multiply the wattage by the number of hours you use the appliance per day.
  3. Divide the total by 1,000 to convert Watts into Kilowatt-hours (kWh).
  4. Multiply by your local utility's rate per kWh to find your daily cost.
